# Camera Studio

1. Open **Shots → Path**. Compose a view and press **K** to capture it, or select a subject and generate a camera move. The move creates editable camera keys and a cut at the playhead.
2. Open **Effects** and choose **Camera** for the camera's default settings or **Current cut** for a cut-specific version. **Preview Effects** shows that scope. **Path → Preview Cut** plays the camera sequence.
3. Choose a lens preset or open **Lens controls** to adjust distortion, fringing, diffusion, streaks, grain, vignette, and letterbox. For focus, use a distance or select a subject to track. The viewport's Draft setting skips depth of field.
4. Choose a shake rig and adjust strength, speed, and roll. Variation sets the repeatable pattern. To place an impact, whip, crash zoom, flash, glitch, fade, or rack focus, set the playhead and click **Add** under **Timed Effects**. Click an effect to edit its start, duration, and strength.
5. Open **Cuts** to arrange the sequence. A cut inherits its camera's effects until you edit **Current cut**. **Use Camera Effects** clears the override. A fade-out holds its color until another fade or a different effects configuration takes over. Rack focus holds its destination distance.

PNG, PNG sequence, MP4, and WebM use the same camera-effect evaluation as the camera preview.
