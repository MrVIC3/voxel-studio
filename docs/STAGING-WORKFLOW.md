# Staging and animation

Select a character and press **M** to create or select its Movement control. Its transform track is separate from the character's pose keys. Use **G** to move the control and **I** to key it. Adding the control preserves existing animation.

Press **P** and click the scene to draw a path. Choose a duration and apply the waypoints. **Face the path** turns the actor along it. **Add walk cycle** replaces pose keys in the path interval. Leave it off to use the pose you already have or apply any action from Motion Library. Actions apply body and face keys by default. Enable **Include clip root motion** when the action's own translation is part of the performance.

- **V** toggles Fly View. Move with **WASD**, change altitude with **Q/E**, boost with **Shift**, look with right-drag, and scroll to change speed.
- **Shift+T** moves the selection two blocks in front of the viewport camera. For a character, it uses the Movement control.
- **B** toggles placement on a selected build. **Shift+B** starts an empty build. Place on a face, replace it with the brush block, or remove it. **Esc** exits and **Ctrl+Z** undoes.

Block edits retain the build's grid and block-state palette. Each edit rebuilds the selected mesh, so very large schematics may take longer per click.

**Equipment** adds a pose-following item to either hand and equips a separate helmet, chestplate, leggings and boots. Hand items are ordinary scene objects whose grip transforms can be edited. Armor uses textures from the selected Java resource set. Use **Face → Fit to Skin** to change eye and mouth width, height and placement.

**Surface Effects** adds emissive glow, a rim light and a view-dependent edge outline. Use bloom for a halo. **Shots → Lighting by Shot** stores the current scene look or a quick preset for the active cut. The snapshot switches global lighting in Camera Preview and export. Scene light objects keep their own settings.

## Infinity Forest reference study

These observations come from samples across the supplied 26-minute film.

| Sample | Staging to reproduce |
| --- | --- |
| 0:54–2:34 | Warm and cool close-ups cut against a wider forest shot. Stage the actor's movement separately and key brows and gaze for the reaction. |
| 3:31–5:23 | Tight worried expressions give way to overhead movement. Lay out the route in top view and cut wide to show its direction. |
| 7:41 and 11:50 | Pale environments alternate close silhouettes and high overhead angles. Try Whiteout and keep actor color and silhouette readable. |
| 18:00–21:40 | Red effects and fast movement punctuate the action. Try a Crimson Impact cut with timed sparks or a ring and localized prop glow. |
| 22:45–25:00 | Warm silhouettes and tight facial reactions follow the effects-heavy sequence. Try Golden Hour for the wide, then frame the eyes and brows for the insert. |

These are visual observations from the film. Squared Media describes Alex & Steve as a director-led series with individual creative control. [Squared Media’s series page](https://squaredmedia.org/alex-and-steve/)
